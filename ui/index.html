Prior freight experience

Years you declare above are credited to each division you tick, up to a cap — that is what every carrier's hiring bar is judged against. This used to be a text box, and the division names have to match exactly to count, so anything typed a little differently scored nothing and said nothing about it.

Freight you will NOT haul

The company will respect this: these loads are kept off your board, carriers that run this freight as their main business will not be offered, and you will never be asked to swap onto one of these trailers. Placarded freight is not on this list — that is governed by the hazmat classes you hold, below.

HazMat classes you already hold

Starting a fresh ATS profile? Leave these alone. ATS gives you no ADR to begin with — you unlock the classes as you level, and you tell the app on the Career tab as you do. Tick them here only if you are bringing a profile that already has them.

Classes, not a CDL endorsement, because that is what ATS actually gates freight on. A tanker is a trailer and what gates it is what is inside — a fuel tanker is class 3, a gas tanker class 2, a food-grade tanker nothing at all. Doubles and triples are a trailer configuration, not a licence. Class 3 is most fuel haulage and class 8 most chemicals, so those two open the most freight.

Probation

Everyone here starts on probation. Refusing it with less than five years of experience is an automatic decline.
